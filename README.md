# 🍽️ Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard — Excel

> **An interactive Excel dashboard for exploring Swiggy sales, orders, customer ratings, food categories, restaurants, and geographic performance.**

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive sales analysis dashboard built entirely in Microsoft Excel**.

The dashboard transforms raw food-ordering data into a visual report that helps users quickly understand:

- Overall sales and order performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Sales by food type
- Sales across states and cities
- Day-of-week sales patterns
- Top-performing restaurants
- Top-performing food items
- Average order value and customer ratings

The goal is to turn raw transactional data into **clear, interactive, and decision-oriented insights**.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- 💰 What are the total sales and total number of orders?
- 📈 How do sales change from month to month?
- 🍗 Which food type contributes more to sales?
- 🗺️ Which states and cities generate the most sales?
- 📅 Which days of the week have the highest sales?
- 🏆 Which restaurants are the top performers?
- 🍔 Which food items generate the most sales?
- ⭐ What is the average customer rating?
- 🧾 What is the average order value?

## 🔍 Dashboard Highlights

### 📈 Monthly Sales Trend

Tracks sales performance across months and makes it easier to identify changes and fluctuations in monthly revenue.

### 🍗 Sales by Food Type

Compares **Veg vs Non-Veg** sales using a donut chart.

- Non-Veg: **64%**
- Veg: **36%**

### 🗺️ Geographic Sales Analysis

The dashboard uses a map to visualize sales performance across Indian states and highlights geographical differences in sales.

### 📅 Sales by Day of Week

Compares sales across the week and helps identify high-performing days.

### 🏆 Top 5 Restaurants

Highlights the five restaurants contributing the highest sales.

### 🏙️ Top 5 Cities

Shows the leading cities by sales and provides a quick view of geographical sales concentration.

### 🍔 Top 5 Food Items

Identifies the five food items with the highest sales contribution.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- **Non-Veg orders contribute a larger share of sales** than Veg orders.
- **Saturday records the highest sales** among the displayed days of the week.
- **Bengaluru appears as the leading city** among the displayed top five cities.
- The dashboard shows noticeable differences in sales performance across **states, cities, restaurants, and food items**.

These insights demonstrate how an interactive dashboard can convert transactional data into information that is easier to interpret and communicate.
